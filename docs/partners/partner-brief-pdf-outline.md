# Partner Brief — Ready-to-Send PDF Outline

This document specifies the layout, section headings, callouts, and production
checklist for the one-page professional partner briefs. Hand this to a
designer (or use it directly in Canva / Figma / Google Slides exported to PDF)
to produce a consistent family of briefs: one per market, all sharing the same
skeleton.

The goal is a single page a busy agent, developer, or ecosystem lead can scan
in 60 seconds and know (a) what HomePi Hub is, (b) what's in it for them, and
(c) what to do next.

---

## 1. Page setup and design notes

- **Format:** One page, portrait. Produce both A4 (Kenya, Spain/EU) and
  US Letter (US) exports from the same layout.
- **Margins:** 15–18 mm all around; nothing load-bearing in the outer 10 mm.
- **Type:** One sans-serif family, three sizes only — headline (~24 pt),
  section headers (~13 pt bold), body (~9.5–10.5 pt). No more.
- **Color:** One brand accent color plus neutrals. Accent is reserved for the
  header band, benefit-box borders, and the CTA button. Body stays dark on
  light for print friendliness.
- **Density rule:** Maximum ~250 words of body copy on the page. Everything
  else is headings, callouts, and the CTA. If a section runs long, cut it —
  the brief sells the conversation, not the contract.
- **File weight:** Final PDF under 2 MB so it sends cleanly over email and
  WhatsApp.

## 2. Section-by-section layout (top to bottom)

### Block A — Header band (full width, ~15% of page)

- Logo (left) + product name.
- **Title:** "HomePi Hub — [Market] Professional Partner Brief"
- **Subtitle / tagline (one line):** market-specific, e.g.
  "Reach Pi-native investors with a compliant, Pi-powered property marketplace."
- Accent-color background; white text.

### Block B — "Who this is for" (left column, icon list)

- 3–4 bullet lines, each with a small icon:
  - Licensed agents and brokerages
  - Property managers and developers
  - Lenders and ancillary services
- Callout style: plain list, no box. This block qualifies the reader in
  five seconds.

### Block C — "What you get" (three benefit boxes, side by side)

Three equal boxes with accent borders, each: bold 3–5 word header + one
sentence. The three benefits are always:

1. **New investor audience** — access to Pi-native and crypto-centric
   investors seeking [market] property.
2. **Pi-based advertising** — sponsored regional visibility and featured
   listings, priced in Pi, no fiat rails required.
3. **Leads and analytics** — contact forms, a lead inbox, and per-listing
   performance metrics.

> Design callout: these three boxes are the visual center of the page.
> If the reader looks at nothing else, they see these.

### Block D — "How it works" (numbered steps, full width)

Four numbered steps in a horizontal strip:

1. Apply with your license / registration details
2. Complete verification (KYC)
3. Publish listings and choose ad products
4. Receive and manage leads

Keep each step to a 3–6 word label plus optional one-line caption.

### Block E — Compliance & trust strip (thin band, small type)

One or two lines, visually quiet but present — this is a differentiator with
professional audiences:

> "HomePi Hub verifies professional identity and licensing, applies
> risk-based monitoring, and operates as a technology and advertising
> platform — not a broker, escrow, or fiduciary."

### Block F — "Why now" (two stat callouts, left) + CTA (right)

- **Why now:** two short market-specific hooks (e.g., "Growing crypto
  investor appetite for real-world assets" / "First-mover exposure in the Pi
  ecosystem").
- **CTA box (accent color):**
  - Button-styled text: "Apply as a [Market] partner"
  - URL (short, memorable) + QR code
  - Contact line: name, email, Telegram/WhatsApp handle

### Block G — Footer (one line, smallest type)

Legal line: entity name, "Not a brokerage or financial advisor," version tag
and date (e.g., `v1.0 — Jul 2026`).

## 3. Variant matrix — what changes per market

The skeleton above stays fixed. Only these fields change per variant:

| Field | US | Kenya / East Africa | Spain / EU | Pi ecosystem | Tokenization partners |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Subtitle | "Reach Pi-native investors with a compliant, Pi-powered marketplace." | "Showcase East African projects to global Pi-powered investors." | "Bring Spanish and EU assets to a global Pi and crypto investor base." | "Real-estate deal flow for Pi-native users and apps." | "Bridge real-world real estate to compliant tokenization flows." |
| "Who this is for" | State-licensed agents, brokerages, lenders | Registered agents, land developers, project sponsors | Agencies, developers, coastal/residential specialists | Pi App developers, KYC/identity providers, infra partners | RWA tokenization platforms, legal/structuring partners |
| Benefit box 1 | New investor audience for US property | Global reach for Nairobi/Mombasa/regional projects | Foreign demand for Spanish/EU assets | A concrete Pi-native real-world-asset use case | Pre-qualified property pipeline ready for tokenization |
| "How it works" step 1 | State license number + brokerage affiliation | Local registration/licensing + company details | Company details + local licensing norms | Integration scoping call | Deal-flow and standards alignment call |
| "Why now" hooks | RWA appetite among crypto investors; Pi distribution | Global demand for African growth markets; no Pi-native gateway exists | Sustained foreign demand; early-mover Pi advantage | Ecosystem needs flagship RWA apps | Investor demand for compliant RWA rails |
| Compliance strip emphasis | Fair housing + state advertising rules | Risk-based KYC, cross-border monitoring | EU-standard KYC and data protection | Identity/KYC integration points | Compliance-gated tokenization flags |
| Paper size | Letter | A4 | A4 | A4 + Letter | A4 + Letter |

## 4. Production checklist

Before any brief goes out:

- [ ] Copy fits the ~250-word body budget; no orphan lines.
- [ ] Market variant fields all swapped (no US license language in the Kenya
      brief, etc.).
- [ ] QR code tested on a phone; short URL resolves to the correct
      market-specific application/landing page.
- [ ] Contact details current (email + Telegram/WhatsApp).
- [ ] Compliance strip present and unedited from the approved wording.
- [ ] Footer version tag updated (`v1.x — MMM YYYY`).
- [ ] Exported as PDF/A or standard PDF, < 2 MB, fonts embedded.
- [ ] File named: `homepi-hub-partner-brief-[market]-v[version].pdf`
      (e.g., `homepi-hub-partner-brief-kenya-v1.0.pdf`).
- [ ] Final proofread by a second person for the target market (local
      terminology, spelling conventions).

Store the source design file and each exported PDF together; when the
pricing matrix or compliance wording changes, bump the version on all
variants at once so no stale brief circulates.
