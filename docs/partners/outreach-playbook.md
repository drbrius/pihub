# Partner Outreach Playbook

The operating manual that connects the partner briefs
(`partner-brief-pdf-outline.md`) and the email templates
(`outreach-emails.md`) into a repeatable supply-acquisition motion. Written
so a founder, ops hire, or VA can run it without further instruction.

---

## 1. Objective and targets

**Goal:** recruit a founding cohort of verified professionals per market
before public launch, so the marketplace has credible supply on day one.

Suggested founding-cohort targets (adjust to capacity):

| Market | Founding target | Definition of "done" |
|---|---|---|
| US (1–2 metros) | 15 verified agents/brokerages | KYC complete + ≥3 live listings each |
| Kenya / East Africa | 15 agencies/developers | KYC complete + ≥3 live listings each |
| Spain / EU | 10 agencies | KYC complete + ≥3 live listings each |
| Pi ecosystem | 3 integration partners | Signed scope or live integration |
| Tokenization | 2 partners | Standards alignment agreed in writing |

**Founding-partner offer:** decide one concrete, time-boxed incentive before
outreach starts (e.g., 6 months of featured placement free, or preferential
sponsored-region pricing locked for 12 months). Every email references it;
it must be real and consistent.

---

## 2. The sequence (all segments)

| Day | Touch | Channel | Asset |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | First contact | Email (Template A–E by segment) | Matching partner brief PDF |
| 3–4 | Bump | Same email thread (Template F) | — |
| 7 | Channel switch | Market-preferred channel (see §3) | Link to brief |
| 12–14 | Breakup | Email thread (Template G) | Application link |

Rules:

- Four touches maximum, then the lead goes to **Dormant** for 90 days.
- A reply — even a "no" — stops the sequence immediately; move the lead to
  the appropriate stage (§5) and respond like a human, not a sequence.
- Positive replies get a booking link or direct scheduling within one
  business day. Speed-to-reply is the single biggest controllable factor in
  conversion.

---

## 3. Channel strategy per market

- **US:** email first; day-7 switch is a LinkedIn connection request with a
  two-line note. Agents live in their inbox and on LinkedIn; cold calls are
  low-yield without a local presence.
- **Kenya / East Africa:** email opens the record, but **WhatsApp is the
  working channel** — move there as soon as you have a number. The day-7
  touch is a short WhatsApp message with the brief PDF attached directly.
  Voice notes are normal and effective once contact is established.
- **Spain / EU:** email in Spanish where possible; day-7 switch is a phone
  call during late morning (10:30–13:30 local). Agencies still answer their
  phones.
- **Pi ecosystem:** Telegram and the developer channels where Pi builders
  already congregate. Email is the fallback, not the lead channel.
- **Tokenization partners:** email + LinkedIn; these are B2B/legal-adjacent
  conversations and expect a paper trail.

## 4. Objection handling

Keep answers short, honest, and consistent with the platform's compliance
positioning. Never improvise claims about Pi's value or future.

**"Pi isn't real money / I can't pay bills with Pi."**
> Fair — treat it as a marketing channel, not a treasury decision. The
> founding offer means you pay little or nothing to test it. If the leads
> aren't real, you've lost a few minutes of setup. The investor pool on Pi
> is real and nobody else is marketing to it.

**"Another portal? I already pay [incumbent]."**
> We're not asking you to leave anything. This is an incremental audience —
> crypto-native and diaspora investors who aren't searching the incumbent
> portals. Your listings are already made; publishing them here is minutes
> of work.

**"What does it cost?"**
> Free to list at launch for founding partners. Paid products are optional
> visibility upgrades — featured listings and sponsored regions — priced in
> Pi. You'll see the exact pricing table inside the app before you commit
> to anything.

**"Is this compliant? Am I taking a risk?"**
> The platform is a technology and advertising service — we're not a
> broker, we never touch client funds, and there's no escrow. You keep your
> existing legal duties, and we verify every professional (KYC + license
> checks) so you're not sharing a marketplace with scammers. I can send the
> Trust & Safety summary and the professional agreement before you sign
> anything.

**"How many investors do you actually have?"**
> Honest answer at this stage: we're pre-launch and building both sides.
> That's exactly why the founding offer exists — you get free, prominent
> placement in exchange for being early. Early partners get outsized
> visibility that won't be available later.

**"I need to ask my broker / partners."**
> Perfect — the one-page brief is written for exactly that. Want me to join
> a 10-minute call with them, or send the professional agreement so your
> counsel can look first?

**Red-line rule:** never promise lead volumes, transaction outcomes, or
anything about the future value of Pi. If a prospect will only sign for a
guarantee, they are not a founding partner — let them go.

## 5. Pipeline stages and tracking

Track in a spreadsheet or lightweight CRM. One row per prospect; these
columns:

| Column | Notes |
|---|---|
| Name / Company | |
| Segment | US agent / KE developer / ES agency / Pi eco / Tokenization |
| Market / Region | Metro, county, or province |
| Source | List research, referral, event, inbound |
| Contact channel(s) | Email, WhatsApp, Telegram, LinkedIn |
| Stage | New → Contacted → Bumped → Switched → Replied → Call booked → Applied → Verified → Live (≥3 listings) → Dormant / Closed-lost |
| Last touch date | Drives the day-3/7/14 cadence |
| Next action + date | Every open row must have one |
| Founding offer given? | Y/N + which |
| Notes | Objections raised, personal context |

**Weekly KPIs** (review every Monday, 30 minutes):

- Sends, reply rate (target ≥8–10% cold; below 5% after 50 sends = rewrite
  the template or fix the list)
- Positive-reply → call-booked conversion (target ≥50%)
- Call → application conversion (target ≥40%)
- Application → verified (this measures your own KYC ops, not the prospect
  — if it stalls, fix onboarding, not outreach)
- Verified → live with 3+ listings (activation; chase with a concierge
  "we'll upload your first listings for you" offer if it lags)

---

## 6. Handoff into onboarding

The moment a prospect says yes, outreach's job ends and onboarding's
begins — with no gap:

1. Send the application link within one business day, plus what they'll
   need: license/registration details, ID or company documents, service
   regions.
2. Their application enters the professional onboarding SOP: intake → KYC
   and license verification → risk-tier assignment → approval and account
   configuration.
3. First listings from new accounts go through the initial content review
   before publishing — tell partners this up front ("your first listings
   are reviewed within 48 hours") so it reads as quality control, not
   friction.
4. Once live, schedule a 2-week check-in: listings performing? Lead inbox
   understood? Introduce paid products only after they've seen the free
   experience work.

**Concierge rule for the founding cohort:** offer to upload each founding
partner's first three listings for them from materials they email over.
Activation is the bottleneck that kills marketplaces — pay it down manually
while the cohort is small.

---

## 7. Cadence of the motion itself

- **Daily (30–45 min):** send new first-touches, clear due bumps/switches,
  reply to everything from the last 24h.
- **Weekly:** KPI review (§5), template tweaks (change one variable at a
  time), refill the prospect list to keep ≥50 open rows per active market.
- **Monthly:** retire what isn't working (a market, a template, a channel),
  refresh the briefs' version if pricing or compliance wording changed, and
  wake the 90-day Dormant list with a single new-angle touch (product
  milestone, new market stat, launch date).
