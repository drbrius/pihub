# Partner Outreach — Email Templates

Templates for first-touch and follow-up outreach to prospective supply-side
partners. Every template follows the same rules:

- **Under ~150 words.** Cold readers skim; the brief and the call carry the
  detail.
- **One CTA only** — either "reply" or "book a 15-minute call," never both.
- **Attach or link the matching partner brief** (see
  `partner-brief-pdf-outline.md`) — the email sells the open, the brief sells
  the conversation.
- Personalization slots are marked `[LIKE_THIS]`. Fill every slot or delete
  the sentence; a half-personalized email reads worse than a plain one.

## Sending rules

- **Volume:** keep initial sends small (10–20/day per market) until reply
  rates prove the template; this also protects sender reputation.
- **Sequence:** first touch → bump (day 3–4) → channel switch (day 7, see
  playbook) → breakup (day 12–14). Never more than four touches.
- **Never** promise returns, guaranteed leads, or Pi price appreciation —
  this violates the platform's own advertising standards and creates
  regulatory exposure. Sell *exposure and tools*, not outcomes.
- Log every send and reply per the tracking sheet in
  `outreach-playbook.md`.
